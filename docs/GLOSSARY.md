# Factor research glossary

- **Signal date**: date when a factor score or target weight becomes known.
- **Rebalance date**: scheduled date selected by the strategy for acting on a signal.
- **Execution date**: trading date when the target is actually traded after its configured delay.
- **Forward return**: return beginning after the signal date and ending at a declared future horizon.
- **Information coefficient (IC)**: cross-sectional correlation between factor scores and forward returns.
- **ICIR**: mean IC divided by the standard deviation of IC.
- **Turnover**: absolute traded notional divided by portfolio equity; factor turnover measures membership replacement instead.
- **Gross exposure**: sum of absolute portfolio weights.
- **Net exposure**: sum of signed portfolio weights.
- **Transaction cost**: commission plus slippage applied to absolute traded notional.
- **Neutralization**: cross-sectional regression that removes industry or continuous exposure effects.
- **Walk-forward test**: repeated training on earlier complete dates and prediction on later complete dates.
- **Survivorship bias**: distortion caused by researching only securities known to survive or remain listed later.
